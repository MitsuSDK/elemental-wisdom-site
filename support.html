Frequently asked

I’m not getting notifications.

Notifications are part of Companion Mode. Make sure you’ve unlocked it, then check that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings → Elemental Wisdom → Notifications. In the app, open Settings and confirm your greeting times are switched on.

The widget isn’t updating.

Widgets are part of Companion Mode. After unlocking, add the widget from your home or lock screen, then open the app once so it can share the latest lines. The widget follows the weather, so it changes through the day.

How do weather lines work?

With your permission, the app checks the approximate weather where you are and picks a matching line. Your location is used only for that and never stored.

I bought Companion Mode but it’s locked on my other device.

Open Settings and tap “Restore Purchases.” Companion Mode is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID and works on all your devices.

Is this an official app?

No. Elemental Wisdom is an independent, unofficial fan app made out of love for the characters. See the note below.
