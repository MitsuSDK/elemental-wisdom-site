Privacy Policy

Elemental Wisdom · Last updated 8 July 2026

Elemental Wisdom (“the app”) is designed to respect your privacy. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, and no analytics or advertising. Everything the app remembers stays on your device.

Information the app uses

Approximate location. If you enable weather features, the app requests your device’s approximate (coarse) location to look up current weather conditions and choose a fitting character quote. If you turn on weather notifications, a rounded location (accurate to about 11 km) is saved on your device so the app can check the forecast later. It is removed if you delete the app, is never uploaded to us, and is shared only with the weather service described below.

Your preferences. Your chosen favorite characters, notification times, birthday, and settings are saved locally on your device only. They are never uploaded.

Weather data

To determine current and forecast conditions, the app sends your approximate coordinates to the Open-Meteo weather service (open-meteo.com). Open-Meteo returns weather data and does not require an account or identify you. No other information is sent.

Purchases

The optional one-time “Companion Mode” purchase is handled entirely by Apple’s App Store or Google Play and processed through RevenueCat, which manages the purchase receipt so the app knows the feature is unlocked. We do not see or store your payment details. Apple’s, Google’s, and RevenueCat’s own privacy policies govern that transaction.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal information, because we do not collect any.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
